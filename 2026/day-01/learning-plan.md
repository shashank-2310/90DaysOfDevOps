## 🧠 My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud
**DevOps** is about building reliable systems through automation, collaboration, and ownership — from code to production.  
**Cloud Engineering** is about designing scalable, resilient infrastructure that supports fast and safe software delivery.

Together, they enable:
- Faster releases
- Fewer failures
- Better sleep 😄
